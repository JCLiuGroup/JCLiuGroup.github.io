Prospective Students & Postdocs

We are always looking for talented and motivated individuals to join our research group.

If you are interested in pursuing graduate studies or postdoctoral research with us, please send the following to ljc@nankai.edu.cn:

  • Your CV/Resume
  • Brief description of your research interests
  • Academic transcripts (for prospective students)
  • List of publications (for postdoc applicants)
  • Contact information for 2-3 references
