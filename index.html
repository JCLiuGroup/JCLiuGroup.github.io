About Our Group

The JC Liu Research Group is part of the School of Materials Science and Engineering at Nankai University. We are dedicated to advancing the understanding and development of novel materials for energy and environmental applications.

Our interdisciplinary team combines expertise in materials science, chemistry, and engineering to tackle some of the most pressing challenges in energy storage and sustainable technologies.

We welcome collaborations with researchers and industry partners who share our passion for materials innovation.
